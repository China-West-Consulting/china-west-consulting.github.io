Discover Cantonese Opera

Explore Cantonese Opera (粵劇), a jewel of Lingnan culture. This dynamic art blends history, music, and drama, featuring:

  • Elaborate Costumes & Makeup: Visual storytelling through silk, embroidery, and symbolic face paint.
  • Unique Musicality: Powerful orchestra, distinctive percussion, soaring vocals, and traditional melodies.
  • Mythic Storytelling: Grand narratives of history, legend, romance, and virtue.
  • Dynamic Performance: Masterful acting, stylized movement, acrobatics, and martial arts.
  • Fully Accessible: Clear English supertitles ensure you follow every moment.

Our Commitment to Culture

Jing Ying Cantonese Opera Institute Founded 1997 in SF's Chinatown, preserves and promotes Cantonese opera heritage.

Led by founder & renowned Erhu master Xiu Ben Chen, we bridge cultures and generations through performance and education, sharing this vibrant art form.
