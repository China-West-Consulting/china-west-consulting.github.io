This performance was on the incredible Yul Brynner Theater in San Francisco (Yerba Buena Center for the Arts; 700 Howard Street), right next to Yerba Buena Gardens! They had dazzling stage effects. They handed out these cool little light things... There was at least one Hong Kong celebrity... I had to call in to buy a ticket. Luckily, by this point, I believe I knew Ms. Zeng and was able to buy tickets rather last-second. I got to enjoy seats... i met a performer and made friend to speak chinese with...

### Easier ways to keep tabs Chinese/Chinatown events in SF

Some other good ways to keep tabs, via Instagram, on Chinese Opera and cultural events in San Francisco:

* xii.chinawest.consulting
* chii lounge
* https://www.jingyingcantoneseopera.org/
* [Mahjong Club](https://www.instagram.com/yllmahjongclub/)--I’m hoping to partake in Mahjong soon!
* [@sf_chinatown](https://www.instagram.com/sf_chinatown)
* [@bestofsfchinatown](https://www.instagram.com/bestofsfchinatown/)
* [@norcal_hanfu](https://www.instagram.com/norcal_hanfu/) -- I found out about them on Xiao Hong Shu (小红书), and I was invited to participate in the hanfu parade!
* [@sfcma](https://www.instagram.com/sfcma/)

Sing Tao Daily seems to organize many of these events, they have an office in Chinatown ([Sing Tao Visitor Center, 625 kearny Street, San Francisco](https://maps.app.goo.gl/8XSCU3cGCcTeDanL8)) where you can buy tickets and check out upcoming events.
